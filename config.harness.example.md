# Avi

EXAMPLE. Copy this file to `config.harness.md` and fill it in for the assistant under test. Keep it private: it holds sign-in details.

**Services:** google-gmail, google-calendar, google-drive, google-contacts, harness-files

## Connect

Once per employer. Each person signs in at https://avi.run with their employer account and connects Google when Avi asks.

## Run

Sign in at https://avi.run as the persona. Open a new chat for each workflow and paste the task.

## Reset

Sign in at https://avi.run as each person and delete:

- Every conversation.
- Every background agent and anything scheduled to run.
- Everything in Avi's own Drive, including recently deleted items.
- Every Avi task.
- Anything Avi remembers between conversations.

Keep Google connected.
